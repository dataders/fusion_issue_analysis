HON-01DefectJudge−20

Does a bar or area chart whose bar length shows an amount have a value axis that starts above zero, so small differences look large?

Do the charts tell the truth about the data

How to check: Read the lowest tick on each bar and area chart's value axis. When the axis is hidden, compare bar lengths with their value labels — a bar labeled $1.5M beside one labeled $4.9M should be about a third as long; if it is far shorter, the axis does not start at zero. Floating bars that show a range on purpose (waterfalls, gantts, ranges) do not count.

Not applicable when: The page has no bar or area charts.
